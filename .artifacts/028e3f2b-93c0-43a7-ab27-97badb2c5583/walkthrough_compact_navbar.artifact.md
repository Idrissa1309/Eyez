# Walkthrough - Compact Modern Navigation Bar

I have successfully reduced the size of the bottom navigation bar to make it more discreet while preserving its premium curved aesthetic.

## Changes Made

### 1. Compact Dimensions
- **Height Reduction**: The total height of the navigation bar container has been reduced from **90px to 80px**.
- **Slimmer Profile**: The main horizontal bar is now only **55px** high, giving it a much more modern and less intrusive look.
- **Lower Wave**: The central "wave" curve is now shallower (**18px** peak), ensuring it still cradles the Eye button without taking up too much vertical space.

### 2. Balanced Iconography
- **Rescaled Icons**: Reduced the icon sizes from **28pt to 24pt** for the side items (Home, Profile) and from **32pt to 28pt** for the central Eye logo.
- **Proportional Spacing**: Adjusted the internal padding and margins to ensure perfect vertical centering within the new smaller frame.

### 3. Visual Refinement
- **Fine Lines**: Thinned the border stroke and reduced the glow spread to match the new compact scale, maintaining the high-fidelity neon feel without it looking "oversaturated".
